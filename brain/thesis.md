# Investment Thesis

## Hard Filters
- Stage: pre-seed or seed only
- Raised: under $10M total
- Team size: under 20 employees
- Founded: within the last 18 months
- Must have: some form of early traction (beta users, waitlist, quiet launch, paying customers)
- Must NOT have: recent press coverage, TechCrunch/Forbes mentions, Crunchbase news entries

## Soft Scoring Criteria
- Founder pedigree (prior top company, repeat founder, strong eng background)
- AI-native product (AI is the core, not a wrapper)
- Obscurity level (harder to find = better)
- Multi-source triangulation (found on 2+ independent sources = conviction bonus)
- Early traction signals (beta, waitlist, Show HN, quiet PH launch)
- Warm intro potential

## What We're Optimizing For
Booked calls with promising founders. Not deal volume. Not press hits.
The best startup is one with a strong founder, early traction, and nobody else has called them yet.
