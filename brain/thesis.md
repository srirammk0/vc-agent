# Investment Thesis

## Hard Filters (must pass all)
- Stage: pre-seed or seed only
- Raised: under $10M total
- Team size: under 20 employees
- Founded: within the last 18 months
- Category: AI-native (AI is the product, not a feature)

## Soft Criteria (used for scoring)
- Founder pedigree: prior YC, top-tier eng background, repeat founder
- Accelerator signal: YC, South Park Commons, Founders Inc, AI Grant
- Traction: waitlist, paying customers, ProductHunt launch, press
- Market: infra, dev tools, vertical AI, agents — preferred
- Geo: SF, NYC, remote-first — preferred

## Sources to Monitor
- YC: ycombinator.com/companies, X @ycombinator
- South Park Commons: southparkcommons.com, X @southparkcommons
- Founders Inc: X @foundersinc
- Keywords: "pre-seed", "just raised", "stealth mode", "building in public", "launched today", "seed round"
